# AI Native Sales — points we're making

**Theme:** AI Native Sales — ways to sell with AI. **Argument:** escape the
endless AI productivity loop. The enumerated claims this video makes. Each should
trace back to a premise in [argument.md](./argument.md) and, ideally, a proof
point in its ledger. See [ai-sales-loop.md](./ai-sales-loop.md) for the loop
that replaces the productivity treadmill.

0. **Most people are stuck in the AI productivity loop.** They point AI at the
   work they already do — faster emails, more drafts, more output — and the
   revenue line never moves. Productivity has no finish line; the gains get
   competed away. The escape is to point AI at the *sell side*, not your to-do
   list. *(Problem / overarching)*

1. **The audience already flipped.** As of April 2026, bots are the majority of
   web traffic (~56–57% of requests). You're building for a room that's more than
   half machines. *(Problem)*

2. **Crawlers and agents are not the same thing.** A crawler reads your page; an
   agent *does the task* — including the buying. The fast-growing slice is
   agentic AI acting on a person's behalf (~8,000% growth in 2025). *(Mechanism)*

3. **Your next customer arrives with an API key and a budget, not a browser.**
   The buyer has intent and money but no eyeballs — so visual funnels, hero
   images, and button colors are aimed at someone who isn't in the room.

4. **The conversion mechanism is not only the click — it's the conversation.**
   Agents don't scan a landing page and click "Buy." They *negotiate the task* in
   natural language: ask what you do, what it costs, whether you fit the job, then
   call and pay. You convert by being legible and answerable in dialogue — your
   pricing, capabilities, and terms have to survive being *asked about*, not just
   *looked at*.

5. **A human storefront is the wrong storefront.** The right one is a metered
   contract an agent can discover, call, and settle per use — signup, usage, and
   payment all machine-legible. *(Vehicle)*

6. **Discoverability moves from SEO to agent-legibility.** You don't rank on
   Google for an agent — you get found, understood, and trusted by a model. The
   new "above the fold" is a clear contract and a clean description.

7. **Being early is the entire edge.** The person who watches ~20% of internet
   traffic predicted this crossover for 2027; it landed ~18 months early. Waiting
   is a bet against the trend line. *(Urgency)*

8. **The win condition changes.** The builders who win the next cycle aren't the
   ones with the best website — they're the ones who got *bought by an agent*
   first.

9. **Step 1 — go try to buy your own product with an agent.** The single exercise
   that makes all of this real: sit in your customer's chair, tell an agent to buy
   your thing, and watch where it stalls. Wherever it gets stuck — can't find you,
   can't get a straight answer, can't check out — is exactly the opening: fix it and
   you become the business an agent can pick, in a category where almost nobody is
   ready yet. The closing CTA of the video. *(Action)*
