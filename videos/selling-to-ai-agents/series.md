# The series — daily commentary, not tutorials

**Format:** lightweight yap-style video essays, ~5–10 min, one point each, with screen
shares as *evidence* (not instruction). Daily cadence.

**Stance:** agents are a **new opportunity** — a brand-new customer base on top of your
human customers. Land-grab, not funeral. Keep the urgency *positive*: the shelf is
empty and you can be first, not "you're bleeding invisible sales."

**The escape from tutorial hell:** lead with the take, let the screen prove it. Tutorials
are the commodity byproduct; your *perspective* is the product. If a title could start
with "How to," rewrite it until it starts with a claim. See [titles.md](./titles.md)
for the voice; the pillar is **"Your Next Customer Is Claude."**

---

## The engine — 3 repeatable franchises

Run these 3–4×/week so you never face a blank page. The format is fixed; only the
subject changes.

### 🅰️ "Can an Agent Buy This?" *(flagship)*
Pick a real product/brand/service and try to buy it live with Claude on screen. Narrate
what happens. The demo does the arguing — and in the additive framing, a *success* is
just as good a video as a stall ("look how easy it is to be the one that gets picked").
Renewable forever, screen-share-native, low prep.

### 🔍 "Agent-Legible or Invisible?"
5-minute teardown of one real site/pricing page from an agent's POV. Can it get a
straight answer? Score it. Frame the gaps as *open lanes*, not failures.

### 🪞 "Behind the Glass"
Show the two conversations — the chat the customer sees vs. the agent↔business exchange
that decides the sale. Your recurring "here's the mechanism" explainer.

## Newsjack lane — comment, don't lecture

Pin takes to what just happened (model launches, the next Cloudflare report, a company
shipping agent checkout) so it reads as commentary, not an evergreen lecture. Skills
that feed this daily: `newsjack-watcher`, `show-producer`, `livestream-producer`.
*"X just shipped Y — here's the new customer it just put in front of you."*

## How it ladders

Franchises 3–4×/week as the base, one arc essay or hot take in between, newsjack when
something breaks. Every "Can an Agent Buy This?" episode naturally seeds an Arc 3–4
point, so the formats feed the essays. Arcs double as **playlists** so the channel reads
as a body of work pointing back to the pillar, **"Your Next Customer Is Claude."**
