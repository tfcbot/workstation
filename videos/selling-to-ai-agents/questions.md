# Selling to AI Agents — questions I raise (solo)

15 questions to pose *to the camera* and then answer myself — the hook for each
beat of the video. No cohost: I bring the question up, let it hang for a second,
then make the point. Ordered to build the argument the way [argument.md](./argument.md)
does. Each maps to a point in [points.md](./points.md) (point # noted).

## Cold open — name the trap

0. Be honest — how are you actually using AI right now? Because if the answer is
   "to get my work done faster," I think you're stuck in a loop that never pays
   off. Let me show you the way out. *(P0)*

## The shift — feel it

1. Here's a question worth sitting with: who's actually visiting your website
   right now? Because the answer flipped, and most people missed it. *(P1)*

2. Cloudflare's own CEO said bots wouldn't pass humans until 2027 — so why did it
   already happen in April 2026? *(P7)*

3. For twenty years the entire game was "get a human to click." So what happens
   the moment the visitor isn't human anymore? *(P3)*

## The turn — crawlers vs. agents

4. Everybody talks about "bot traffic" — but here's the distinction nobody's
   making: what's the difference between a crawler and an *agent*? *(P2)*

5. Let me show you what it actually means when an agent doesn't just read your
   page, but *does the task* — including the buying. *(P2)*

6. So if my next customer shows up with an API key and a budget but no eyeballs…
   what was all that landing-page work even for? *(P3)*

## The big idea — conversation as conversion

7. Here's the line I want you to remember: the conversion mechanism isn't the
   click anymore — it's the conversation. Let me explain. *(P4)*

8. What does it actually mean for your business to be *answerable* — to survive an
   agent asking about your price instead of just looking at it? *(P4)*

9. Picture a great product that an agent walks away from halfway through the
   conversation. Why does it lose? *(P4)*

10. If the funnel is a dialogue now, then what's the new "above the fold"? *(P6)*

## The mechanism — what you actually build

11. So concretely — what does an agent-buyable service have that a normal website
    doesn't? *(P5)*

12. And how does an agent even *find* you if SEO isn't the channel anymore? *(P6)*

13. What's the smallest version of this I could ship this week to land a first
    agent customer? *(P5)*

## The close — why now

14. So what does waiting a year actually cost you here? *(P7)*

15. Let me leave you with this: the builders who win the next cycle aren't the ones
    with the best website — they're the ones who *what*? *(P8)*
