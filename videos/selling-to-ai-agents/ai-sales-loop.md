# The AI Sales Loop

The escape from the AI **productivity** loop. See [argument.md](./argument.md) for
why the productivity loop is a dead end and [points.md](./points.md) for the points
it surfaces.

---

## Two loops, side by side

The productivity loop spins and goes nowhere. The sales loop spins and *compounds*.
The whole video is the move from the left to the right.

### The AI Productivity Loop *(the trap)*

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                       │
        ▼                                       │
  Do your existing work  ──►  Get a little      │
  with AI (faster)            faster / cheaper  │
                                   │            │
                                   ▼            │
                        Everyone gets the       │
                        same tools              │
                                   │            │
                                   ▼            │
                        Gains compete away ─────┘
                        (revenue flat)
```

- **Input:** your own to-do list.
- **Output:** lower cost, more output, same revenue.
- **Why it never ends:** productivity has no finish line, and any edge is
  temporary because the tools are commodity. You optimize forever.

### The AI Sales Loop *(the escape)*

```
        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                                │
        ▼                                                │
  1. Package a skill        2. Expose it as a            │
     as a service     ──►      service agents can  ──►   │
     (sell side)               discover + call           │
                                       │                 │
                                       ▼                 │
                              3. Agents buy via          │
                                 conversation            │
                                 (call + pay per use)    │
                                       │                 │
                                       ▼                 │
        ┌──────────────────  4. Revenue + usage data ────┘
        │                       feed the next service
        ▼
  5. Reinvest into more / better services  ──► (loop tightens)
```

- **Input:** the new buyer (agents) and what they're already paying for.
- **Output:** revenue that compounds — each sale teaches you what to build next.
- **Why it compounds:** you're on the sell side, where AI creates *new* markets
  instead of shaving cost off old work. Early movers own the agent buyer
  relationship before it's contested.

---

## The five stages (sell-side)

1. **Package** — turn a skill you already have into a discrete, sellable service.
2. **Expose** — put it behind a metered contract an agent can discover and call
   (machine-legible pricing, capabilities, terms).
3. **Convert** — the agent *converses*, not clicks: asks what you do, what it
   costs, whether you fit — then calls and pays per use. *(conversion = the
   conversation, points.md P4)*
4. **Learn** — every call is signal: what agents ask for, where they walk away,
   what they'll pay. Revenue + usage data in one stream.
5. **Reinvest** — feed that signal into more and better services. The loop tightens
   instead of flattening.

---

## The one-line contrast (for the video)

> The productivity loop asks *"how do I do my work faster?"* — and the answer
> never moves your revenue. The AI Sales Loop asks *"what can I sell to the new
> buyer?"* — and every turn of the loop makes the next sale easier. One is a
> treadmill. The other is a flywheel.
