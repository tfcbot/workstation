# Title options

Working titles for the video. Top pick first.

1. **Your Next Customer Is Claude, Not Another Human** ⭐
   Concrete and a little jarring — names the agent instead of abstracting it. Strong
   resonance with a builder/operator audience; trades some reach for sharpness (brand
   naming dates faster, slight ad-vibe). The hook for the *agent-as-buyer* pillar.

## Alternates to A/B against it

- **Your Best Customer Will Never See Your Website** — leans on the silent-loss
  angle; no brand-dating. Great thumbnail / cold-open line even if not the title.
- **I Tried to Buy My Own Product With an AI — It Couldn't** — first-person,
  demo-forward, very video-essay. Pairs with the Step 1 exercise.
- **Stop Selling to Humans** — punchier, more aggressive, less specific.
- **Your Next Customer Is a Robot With a Credit Card** — keeps the concreteness
  without naming a vendor.
