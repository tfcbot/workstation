# Title options

Stance: this is a **new opportunity, not a replacement.** Agents are a brand-new
customer base showing up *on top of* your human customers — a land-grab, not a
funeral. Every title is a bold, definitive, first-person perspective statement — a
line in the sand, never a "how to."

## Pillar / episode 1

**Your Next Customer Is Claude** ⭐
The anchor video the whole series points back to. (Dropped the original
"…Not Another Human" tail — that was replacement framing; additive-by-omission is
bolder and cleaner.)

## The drumbeat — 4 stances to bang on from new angles

2. I'd rather be the product Claude recommends than the one that ranks on Google.
3. The next great sales channel is a conversation, and it's wide open.
4. Getting bought by an agent is the cheapest growth lever in business right now.

## What makes these work

- **Declarative**, not instructional — no "how to," (almost) no questions.
- **First-person / stance-forward** — the channel becomes your worldview.
- **Additive language** — "new," "second kind," "wide open," "up for grabs" — never
  "instead of" or "dead."
